Being passionate about IT and STEM in general for as long as I can remember, I
started my education at the [Katholieke Hogeschool
Leuven](https://www.ucll.be/nl), where I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in
Applied Computer Science in 2013, specialising in software development.
Afterwards, I immediately began my career at [Faros](https://faros.be/), working
as a back-end developer with Java and Spring.

In early 2017, however, I decided to shift my focus to front-end development. I
wanted to be "closer" to the end user and master JavaScript, which was rapidly
gaining popularity across various frameworks. I joined
[Euricom](https://www.euri.com/) to participate in their bootcamp, which focused
on Angular and .NET Core. Although I never got much chance to explore .NET Core,
Angular became my main area of expertise. At the same time, I’ve stayed
interested in the wider front-end ecosystem.

At the end of 2018, together with a friend and colleague, I co-founded
[Appgrade](https://appgrade.be/). Over the years, our company has grown, and we
have welcomed several employees to the team.

Apart from the professional side of things, I like to hike to give my eyes a
rest from all the screens, travel to discover other cultures and food, and play
music. Truth be told, since becoming a father it can sometimes get a bit
overwhelming to combine all these passions, but I wouldn't have it any other
way.
